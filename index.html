Hover the wheel for a color preview. Click anywhere on the wheel to copy that color as HEX.

Drag the endpoint knobs directly around the wheel to change the colors. The colored path is the actual sequence of intermediate colors for the selected mixing model.

The main wheel is HSV: hue is the angle and saturation is the radius. OKLCH is only an optional interpolation model; it does not replace the HSV wheel. Every point on the path is first generated by the selected interpolation method, then converted to HSV and plotted at its actual hue/saturation coordinates. This lets you compare how screen/RGB interpolation differs from perceptual and paint-oriented mixing. CSP itself distinguishes Standard and Perceptual gradient mixing; its Perceptual mode is designed to produce more realistic and vivid mixtures.
